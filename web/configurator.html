      Red-outlined modules are designed once and reused on more than one stage. Rotor and parachute sizes are worked out for a 6.5 m/s landing, a rule of thumb to confirm with the range safety officer (RSO). Masses and drag are assumptions until CAD and weighing replace them. Apogees are vertical-flight upper bounds; real flights land lower. Big reload prices still need vendor quotes.

      Same equations as the Python sim (extended Barrowman margin, 1-D flight from a 1,300 m site, real ThrustCurve.org thrust curves from National Association of Rocketry (NAR) certification data), checked by an automated test to within 0.1% on apogee. Drag is calibrated to Estes' published Alpha III apogee for Taproot and assumed for the ring tail. Rotor descent uses an effective drag coefficient of 1.08 from the blade-element model at 6° pitch, until drone drops measure it. Drift assumes the wind holds all the way up. Prices were checked on Nov 1 to 2, 2026; "estimate" rows are not quotes. NAR model code: 1,500 g liftoff limit, launch winds of 20 mph or less, and certified motors only.
